ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.85B, PHP 8.4.25, event-driven. Button source (watch_button on BOOT/GPIO0):
click = screen off + light sleep; hold 2s = deep sleep. Measured on an AVHzY CT-3 inline on USB (5 V).

--- /app/index.php (event-driven) ---
[ACTIVE] screen RED -- baseline. BOOT: click = sleep, hold 2s = deep sleep.
I (1606) php-esp32: entering event loop
[ASLEEP] screen off + light sleep -- read current; press BOOT to wake.
[ACTIVE] woke -> screen RED.
[DEEP SLEEP] read current; press BOOT to wake (reboot).
woke from DEEP SLEEP (cause=3)

Measured current @5V (whole board):
  active (screen red)      0.0740 A  ~370 mW
  light sleep (screen off) 0.0030 A  ~15 mW   (~25x less than active; RAM retained, instant wake)
  deep sleep               0.0020 A  ~10 mW   (only ~1 mA below light sleep -> board floor dominates)

Conclusion: light sleep is the practical low-power regime; deep sleep's ~1 mA gain isn't worth the full
reboot + lost RAM on this USB-measured board (~2 mA floor from regulators + USB-serial-JTAG).
